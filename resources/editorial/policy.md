## Editorial purpose

Share useful solutions the owner implemented and critical problems they solved, in plain, concise prose. Start with a real engineering question and the specific decision worth sharing. For transferable technical accounts of this kind, omit Monafy branding and promotion and frame the problem as a general data pipeline or application mechanism. Generalize market/price examples where that domain is unnecessary. Preserve technical substance and source-backed scope; source-project attribution can stay in private evidence. Existing anonymous articles may use project_key=null so an article-specific project sidebar does not contradict this framing. Product-specific case studies or explicitly requested tool analyses retain their separately requested scope. A generic tutorial or a test-name taxonomy is not a substitute for an actual problem and solution.

Use the “Concise technical explanation” section of docs/superpowers/notes/writing-voice.md as the canonical writing standard. Preserve supported engineering substance while selecting only the explanation the reader needs. Review criteria are not mandatory public sections. Use article for a focused decision and case_study for a documented problem needing context; no minimum length or padding target applies.

For performance accounts, explain the supported mechanism and cost without inventing measurements. Distinguish illustrative calculations, product claims and actual results, and bot latency from real-user Core Web Vitals. Keep evidence-process caveats privately; put necessary technical limits beside the relevant mechanism.

## Qualification and types

Before drafting, record privately why this warrants a standalone publication: the actual hard problem or constraint, why the usual solution was insufficient, the decision made and its supporting evidence. Routine permission/validation checks, standard GET/POST behavior and ordinary integrations are insufficient on their own; they may support a larger meaningful problem. Length, technical vocabulary, tables or code do not create topic value. If the substance is insufficient, do not draft or invent difficulty/results.

A topic must have a concrete reader question, a meaningful product capability or reusable engineering decision, evidence for its material claims, and a distinct contribution beyond existing stories/drafts. Group related commits into one feature. Maintenance, dependency updates, routine refactors, small fixes and SEO changes alone do not qualify. Apply the same value threshold when revising existing stories: shortening an elementary integration fix does not make it a worthwhile standalone publication. Require a meaningful decision or problem and do not repackage owner-rejected topics under a new title. If no strong topic exists, do not publish. Date matching, withholding missing values, elementary period arithmetic and routine data consistency alone are not standalone subjects. Combining several such checks or adding a table, example or precomputation note does not satisfy topic value. They may support a larger source-backed engineering problem; do not invent scale, difficulty or performance impact. The owner rejected the fund price/return-date topic; do not repackage it.

Use article for a short engineering note grounded in a real solution or decision. Use case_study for a documented problem, decision, tradeoff and observed outcome or stated limitation. Never create both from the same source set. Stable topic keys and source fingerprints must not change to bypass duplicate prevention. Process unhandled scans as backlog; a cursor advance does not mark editorial work complete.

At most one new bilingual topic per run and four per rolling seven days, including removed publications within the active quota window. These are ceilings, not goals. Existing stories should receive a private revision proposal when a duplicate new topic adds no value. Do not overwrite a human edit or republish suppressed content.

## Evidence, writing and privacy

Owner feedback25 October2026 prioritizes removing unnecessary length and deteriorating final sections. Apply the concise writing standard in writing-voice.md to interactive and automated work. The independent reviewer must identify expendable repetition/paragraphs as well as technical errors; source support alone does not justify including a detail. Preserve the actual solution and meaning-changing limits. Editorial design approval remains separate from exact full-revision publication approval.

Every material claim needs private source evidence. Distinguish code behavior, observed production behavior, measurement and inference. Never invent incidents, dialogue, feelings, outcomes, coverage or success rates. A test in source does not establish a passing run. Financial explanations must preserve dates, session, units, currencies, periods and missing-data meaning; correlation does not prove causation. Editorial research uses primary sources when needed.

Use specific, reader-oriented headlines that identify the real question or distinguishing decision. Review titles as a domain specialist: preserve the body's technical/financial scope and do not promise unsupported incidents, measurements or guarantees. Avoid repetitive activity-label headlines and do not force every title into a question. The excerpt must make sense without the body: establish the actual problem or finding instead of merely listing tools and implementation steps. Give necessary technical terms enough context to explain what waited or changed. Write the excerpt as a direct finding and the meta description as a distinct, page-specific search preview; avoid "in this article" or "nasıl yaptığımı anlatıyorum" filler. Published slugs remain unchanged after headline edits. Use natural TR/EN prose. Owner vocabulary preference: avoid insan and its inflections in Turkish public stories, and human in the corresponding English prose. Use gerçek kullanıcı/kullanıcı/oyuncu/okuyucu or real user/player/visitor as appropriate; preserve meaning and natural grammar. Avoid repeated co-founder/engineer introductions; the site bio carries the role. Use direct, process-centered narration in the body and summary instead of habitual first-person I-do/I-check accounts. Name the agent, service, file or task when the actor matters; use natural passive wording where it does not. Do not mechanically turn every sentence passive or imply unverified automation or outcomes. General market wording is Türkiye ve ABD borsaları / Turkish and US stock markets. Keep natural summaries, descriptive locale-specific slugs and short paragraphs. StoryStyle maximum lengths are house ceilings, not ranking rules; there is no minimum and no padding quota.

## Validation and publication

Before collection or research, run `editorial:status --remote` to read the production publication ledger. Stop if local publication permission is disabled, capacity is zero, or status is unavailable. Do not infer quota from live HTML dates, receipt modification times or the local database. Remember the next available slot and avoid repeating unchanged waiting notifications. Limit each eligible run to one deeply researched candidate and a 15-minute wall-clock budget checked between tool calls; persist unhandled backlog and phase timestamps. This budget cannot prevent host sleep or a suspended agent runtime.

Use bounded direct GETs with curl or the Laravel HTTP client for public verification. Python urllib's default signature may receive Cloudflare 403/1010 while normal clients succeed. Compare once and distinguish a client-specific edge block from an application failure; never treat actual HTTP errors as success or disable security checks to make a check pass.

Write bundles and readable review copies under storage/app/private/editorial. Keep evidence outside public HTML. Run editorial:validate, then leave the private revision for owner review. Only an exact owner-approved revision may be published or applied interactively; scheduled runs never publish or update live content. One bounded correction is allowed for a concrete validation/source failure. Unresolved factual or privacy questions leave the package unpublished.

The publish command transfers only the sealed public payload, verifies live pages and retains receipts. Retry the unchanged file after a connection failure. Never regenerate identifiers to force a new post. Verify both locale addresses and public metadata after publication, then prepare external drafts with actual canonical URLs. Do not claim an external draft creates a backlink or guarantee indexing/ranking.

Stay quiet if no new strong topic exists. Notify for a verified publication, meaningful failure or required user action. The local schedule depends on the computer and Codex being running.
