# Concise engineering writing

Approved in chat on 23 October 2026. The owner wants periodic, plain accounts of good solutions they implemented and critical problems they solved, with Monafy appearing naturally through that work.

Use short engineering notes for a focused decision and case studies when a critical problem needs additional context. There is no word minimum. Keep the existing maximum lengths and short-paragraph checks as ceilings. The material determines length; stop when the decision is understood. Explain the problem, its relevant context, the chosen solution and its meaningful tradeoff. Avoid generic tutorials assembled from test names, marketing introductions, and public disclaimers about the research or writing process. Omit unsupported claims; keep evidence questions privately. Retain technical limitations that explain the solution.

Approaches considered: short notes alone would constrain incidents needing context; case studies alone encourage unnecessary expansion. The owner approved combining the two according to the subject.

Apply this to the editorial prompt, policy, voice guide and mechanical validator. Audit all existing public TR/EN stories. Remove process boilerplate where found and rewrite the new testing article around the real decimal-calculation decision visible in its existing evidence. Preserve post identities, slugs, dates, publication history and all unrelated edits. Use existing model validation/content sync for authorized revisions, with exact-content concurrency checks and backups. Recurring runs retain their existing no-code/no-deploy boundary.
